Hi, I’m Shravani Iytha Subramanyam, a Data Analyst with a strong background in finance, auditing, and sales coordination. My professional journey started in the finance and auditing sector, where I analyzed financial data, optimized internal controls, and ensured compliance with regulations. Later, as a Sales and Service Coordinator at Ingersoll Rand, I managed sales data and provided business intelligence to drive strategic decision-making across Europe and North America. Throughout these roles, I developed a keen interest in data-driven insights and the power of analytics to solve business challenges. This passion led me to pivot into data analysis, where I now leverage my expertise in data wrangling, visualization, and machine learning to uncover patterns and provide actionable recommendations.

My goal is to help organizations optimize processes, enhance decision-making, and improve operational efficiency through data-driven strategies.
